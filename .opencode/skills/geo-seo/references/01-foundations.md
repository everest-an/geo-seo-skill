# 01 · GEO Foundations

## 1. What Is GEO

**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** = optimizing content and brand presence so AI-driven search systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Google AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, Grok) **select, cite, summarize, and recommend** it.

Key difference from traditional SEO: SEO competes to "rank at the top of results"; GEO competes to "become part of the final answer" (retrieved → cited → synthesized).

**Related concepts:**
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)**: optimizing for engines that "give the answer directly" (Featured Snippets, voice search, zero-click, AI Overviews). Overlaps heavily with GEO; the label matters less than the mechanics.
- **Agentic Search**: AI not only retrieves but completes tasks on the user's behalf (comparisons, purchases).

## 2. SEO vs GEO vs AEO

| Dimension | SEO | AEO | GEO |
|-----------|-----|-----|-----|
| **Goal** | Rank #1 | Enter the answer box / zero-click | Be cited/recommended by AI |
| **Platform** | Traditional Google/Bing SERP | Answer engines, voice assistants | AI engines (ChatGPT/Perplexity, etc.) |
| **Core tactics** | Crawlability, keywords, intent match, backlinks | Structured answers, Schema, FAQ | Clear extractability, semantic entities, credible mentions |
| **Key metrics** | Keyword rankings, organic traffic | Answer-box share, zero-click rate | AI visibility, citation rate, brand mentions, AI share of voice |

> In one line: **SEO builds the foundation, GEO wins citations, AEO wins answer boxes.** They share much of the same work and should not be separated.

## 4. RAG Retrieval Factors (How AI Selects Content to Cite)

Generative engines work via RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation). Empirical weights during the retrieval stage:

| Factor | Weight | Explanation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Semantic relevance | ~40% | Does content truly answer the query (entities, semantic coverage) |
| Keyword match | ~20% | Still useful, but not decisive |
| Authority signals | ~15% | Source credibility, E-E-A-T, citation frequency |
| Freshness | ~10% | Newer information is prioritized |
| Source diversity | ~15% | Engines prefer multi-source cross-validation |

**Takeaway**: don't just stack keywords — stack "semantic coverage + authority + freshness + cross-validatability."

## 6. Mindset Shift

> For 20 years we optimized for **clicks, rankings, SERP features**.
> Now visibility also means **being cited, summarized, and recommended inside AI-generated answers**.

Key shifts:
1. From "rank" thinking → "be cited" thinking
2. From "keyword density" → "semantic entities + information gain"
3. From "backlink count" → "credible mentions (including unlinked brand mentions)"
4. From "watch only Google" → "watch the full AI engine matrix"
5. From "SEO or GEO, pick one" → "SEO as base + GEO layered on top"
